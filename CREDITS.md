# Haiku (BeOS) Theme for Gmail: credits

The icons in `icons/` are Haiku's own vector icons, converted from the HVIF
data embedded in Haiku's resource files:

- `src/apps/mail/pictures.rdef`, `src/apps/mail/Mail.rdef` (Mail toolbar and app icons)
- `src/kits/tracker/TrackerIcons.rdef` (folders, trash, queries, files)
- `src/apps/haikudepot/HaikuDepot.rdef` (stars, and the green tick of Not Junk)
- The app icons of People, Clock, Expander, StyledEdit, LaunchBox and
  ActivityMonitor (`src/apps/<app>/<App>.rdef`) and of the Notifications
  preferences (`src/preferences/notifications/Notifications.rdef`): the
  pictures for saved queries (people, clock, box, document, rocket, chart,
  megaphone)

Source: https://github.com/haiku/haiku. Copyright Haiku, Inc. and contributors,
distributed under the MIT License (https://github.com/haiku/haiku/blob/master/License.md).

`icons/sidebar.svg` (the folder-list button) is not from Haiku: it was drawn
for this theme in the same style, and is covered by this project's MIT
License.

`icons/back-to-list.svg` (Back to Inbox in an open message) combines two of
Haiku's icons above: the All Mail drawer, with Mail's back arrow over its
bottom-left corner.

`icons/reply-all.svg` is Mail's reply arrow with a letter A added for this
theme.

`icons/query.svg` (query buttons) is Tracker's query drawer with the
magnifying glass from Tracker's query-folder icon over it. `icons/drive.svg`
is Tracker's network-share icon.

`icons/paperclip.svg` (attachments), `icons/plus.svg` (Save Query),
`icons/stopwatch.svg`, `icons/heart.svg`,
`icons/briefcase.svg` (query pictures) and `icons/delete-forever.svg` (the
badge on Trash in Spam and Trash) were drawn for this theme in Haiku's icon
style. `icons/check-mail.svg` is Mail's
envelope with a refresh arrow drawn for this theme in the green of Haiku's
arrows. `icons/clock.svg` is the Clock app's icon with a red exclamation
mark added (Time-sensitive).

`icons/todo.svg` (Task), `icons/person.svg`, `icons/robot.svg` (Automator), `icons/picture.svg` (Mandelbrot),
`icons/recipes.svg` (Birthday Cake), `icons/home.svg`, `icons/caution.svg`, `icons/stop.svg` and
`icons/info.svg` are Haiku's own icons (github.com/darealshinji/haiku-icons, MIT; the
last three also in the GNOME-look conversion).

Gmail is a trademark of Google LLC. This extension only restyles Gmail locally,
in this browser.
